## How to read this artifact

- Each section describes one related-work line in the survey.
- `Representative papers` should be conservative and source-grounded.
- `Problem addressed` explains what this line is trying to solve.
- `Relation to previous stage` explains how the line extends, reorganizes, or reacts to the line before it.
- If the evidence is weak, mark the section or the paper as `tentative`.
